Forty technologies, one form, 30 days

China's commerce and science ministries have proposed adding 40 technologies, 23 of them in AI, to the country's export-control catalogue, and require exporters to register deals from Friday. The draft covers models trained with 10²⁶ or more operations or with two trillion or more parameters. Comments close on 15 November.
