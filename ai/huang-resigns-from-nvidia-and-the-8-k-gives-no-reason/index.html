Huang resigns from Nvidia, and the 8-K gives no reason

Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's chief executive and a director on Wednesday, three days after telling staff he had been unfaithful to his wife. Colette Kress is interim chief executive. Our reading of the filing, and what his 870.6m shares are worth now.

Dana Whitfield · House byline
November 12, 20265 min read
ShareXLinkedInBlueskyFacebookEmail

Item 5.02 of the 8-K that Nvidia filed on Thursday 12 November is four short paragraphs. It records that on Wednesday the board "accepted the resignation of Jen-Hsun Huang" as president, chief executive officer and director, effective immediately, and appointed Colette Kress, the chief financial officer, as interim chief executive. It does not say why he resigned.

Jensen Huang, Nvidia's founding chief executive and a director since 1993, resigned on Wednesday 11 November 2026, three days after telling employees he had been unfaithful to his wife. Kress, 59, will run the company while the board searches for a successor, and Harvey C. Jones, a director since 1993, becomes Nvidia's first chair.

Nvidia's announcement adds little to the filing. The board will consider internal and external candidates, and Kress keeps the finance job in the meantime. Jones, 73, a former chief executive and executive chairman of the chip-design software company Synopsys, said the board has "full confidence in Colette and the leadership team." Kress said: "Our focus remains on serving our customers, partners, developers and shareholders." Third-quarter results will be published as scheduled on Tuesday 17 November, with a call at 2 p.m. Pacific time.

What does the filing leave out?

Most of what investors usually look for in a chief executive's departure. The 8-K does not describe a disagreement or deny one. It mentions no separation agreement or severance, says nothing about Huang's unvested share awards, and gives no figure for Kress's pay as interim chief executive. It does not refer to the inquiry that the independent directors said on Tuesday they had hired Joon H. Kim, a former acting US Attorney for the Southern District of New York, and the law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to conduct. Nor does the press release.

Nvidia's 2026 proxy statement gives some context for the gaps. Under its "What We Don't Do" heading, the company says it does not give executive officers special change-in-control benefits. Huang's pay for fiscal 2026 totalled $36.3m, of which $24.8m was in share awards: two grants of performance share units in March 2025, each with a target of 119,607 shares. Another $4.0m, or 10.9 per cent of the total by our division, was spent on his security. The parts of the proxy that set out what happens to unvested awards when an executive leaves were not available to us in full, and the 8-K does not say. A later filing may.

How much of Nvidia does Huang own?

About 3.58 per cent. The proxy attributes 870,604,104 shares to him at its 27 April record date, most of them through family trusts. At Wednesday's close of $173.73 they were worth $151.3bn, by our multiplication. At the price indicated before Thursday's open they were worth about $135.3bn, $73.0bn less than at Nvidia's record close on 6 October.

How fast did it happen?

Four days from the memo to the filing. Huang told staff on Sunday 8 November that he had been unfaithful to his wife, and the board said that day that independent directors would investigate. Nvidia's shares lost 9.5 per cent over the next two trading days, as Komposite reported when the company shed about $428bn of value. On Wednesday the Wall Street Journal reported that the board was meeting that night to decide his future, according to Yahoo Finance's live coverage. The shares had closed 2.9 per cent higher.

Before the open on Thursday the shares were indicated at $155.40, 10.6 per cent below Wednesday's close, according to Yahoo Finance. At that price Nvidia would be worth about $3.75tn on the 24.1bn shares on its last 10-Q cover, by our arithmetic, against about $5.77tn at the October record. "What matters for the stock from here is less whether a change occurs than how it is executed," Ruben Roy, an analyst at Stifel, said, according to Yahoo Finance.

Kress has been chief financial officer since 2013, and the proxy put her target pay for fiscal 2026 at $16.0m. She will present results on Tuesday as interim chief executive and finance chief at once. The board's choice of Jones, who has sat on it since 1993, as its first chair means the search for Huang's successor will be run by a board that, for the first time, has a chair.

Why it matters: Nvidia's next week was already crowded. Huang had until Wednesday to answer three House committee chairmen who asked him to a closed briefing on 18 November about his dealings with Alice Huang of Megaspeed, the subject of the board inquiry opened 394 days after Megaspeed first surfaced and of a paper trail running from Commerce to the prosecutors. Nvidia has not said whether he, or anyone else from the company, will attend. The results on 17 November will be the first under a chief executive other than its founder.
