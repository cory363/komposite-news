About Komposite News

Komposite News is an independent digital publication covering artificial intelligence, blockchain, digital assets, enterprise technology, cybersecurity, fintech and capital markets. We write for executives, founders, investors, developers and policy professionals who need to understand how technology and finance are changing each other.

Our coverage prioritizes reporting and analysis over hype. We are interested in what is being built, what it costs, who is buying it and what the consequences are, and we hold breathless claims from any direction to the same standard of evidence.

Ownership and funding: Komposite News is independently operated. Any commercial relationship relevant to a story is disclosed within that story, and sponsored content is always clearly labeled. Questions about the publication can be directed through our contact page.
