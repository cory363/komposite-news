Platform engineering becomes the default org chart

Internal developer platforms with paved paths and self-service infrastructure have moved from big-tech pattern to mainstream expectation.

Tom Kessler · Enterprise Technology Reporter
October 14, 2025 · 2 min read
ShareXLinkedInBlueskyFacebookEmail

The internal developer platform, golden paths, self-service infrastructure, and a team that treats other engineers as customers, has completed its journey from big-tech curiosity to mainstream org chart. Enterprises report platform teams as standard structure, and job markets price the specialty accordingly.

The driver is arithmetic: every product team solving infrastructure independently multiplies cost and inconsistency, while a platform amortizes the solutions and bakes in security and compliance where developers cannot forget them. AI raised the stakes by adding model access, evaluation, and cost controls to the list of things best paved once.

The failure mode is equally standardized: platforms built as mandates rather than products, measured by adoption decrees instead of developer satisfaction. The organizations succeeding describe an internal market where the paved path wins by being genuinely faster, which was always the only sustainable enforcement.
